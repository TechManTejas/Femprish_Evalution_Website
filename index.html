Our Services

Education

Providing education and resources for women to learn new skills and advance in their careers.

Safety

Advocating for women's safety and supporting survivors of gender-based violence.

Leadership

Encouraging women to take on leadership roles and empowering them to be decision-makers in their communities.

Healthcare

Advocating for women's reproductive health and access to healthcare, including mental health support.

Women's Health Services

We provide comprehensive health services including gynecological exams, STI testing, and family planning options.

Women's Empowerment Programs

We offer various programs and workshops to help women develop new skills, increase confidence, and achieve their goals.

Legal Assistance

We provide legal support to women who have experienced discrimination, harassment, or domestic violence.

Career Counseling

Our career counselors work with women to develop career goals and create plans for achieving them, as well as offering resources for job search and professional development.

Financial Literacy Training

We offer workshops and resources to help women learn financial management skills and achieve financial stability.

Childcare Services

We offer affordable, high-quality childcare services to support working mothers and promote the well-being of children.

Our Angels
FEMINIST ACTIVIST
Gloria Steinem

Gloria Steinem is an American feminist, journalist, and social and political activist. She became nationally recognized as a leader and a spokeswoman for the feminist movement in the late 1960s and early 1970s.

FEMINIST WRITER
bell hooks

bell hooks is an American author, feminist, and social activist. Her writing focuses on the interconnectivity of race, capitalism, and gender. She is best known for her book "Ain't I a Woman: Black Women and Feminism" which highlights the intersectionality of race and gender in the experiences of Black women.

FEMINIST ICON
Angela Davis

Angela Davis is an American activist, author, and professor who is a prominent figure in the Black feminist and prison abolition movements. She has written extensively on issues such as racism, gender, and class, and is known for her outspoken advocacy of social justice and human rights.
